Why these three made it

SMB activation (any meeting)

Pool 29/qtr · Gap 37.3pp · p = 0.047 · $11,699/qtr · 8 quarters to prove

The only candidate that clears all three tests at once: it pays for itself easily, it's big enough to justify building a real program around, and it's provable within a normal two-year planning horizon. This is where the deck can show full mechanism, process, tooling, and automation depth, because the evidence actually supports building something real. The sharper "no meeting only" slice (57pp gap, p=0.031, provable even faster) is folded in as a detail to watch inside this initiative, not treated as a separate fourth pick, since it's the same underlying population narrowed further.

Paid Search, Lead→PQL

Pool 67/qtr · Gap 20.6pp · p = 0.0026 · $8,141/qtr · 5 quarters to prove

The single strongest statistical case in the whole dataset — already essentially proven with existing data, not just promising — and the biggest volume lever by far. The catch: nobody can define exactly what "PQL" means operationally today. Rather than build automation on an undefined rule, the plan is a cheap, low-risk test plus an explicit, evidence-backed ask to fix the PQL definition itself — protecting the funnel's single biggest leak without overbuilding on a shaky foundation.

Mid-Market, PQL→Trial

Pool 18/qtr · Gap 20.7pp · p = 0.077 · $3,929/qtr · 20 quarters to fully prove

Not yet statistically proven, but the closest of any long-shot candidate to clearing significance, and the only one of the remaining options with a fully worked-out mechanism (an ownership/handoff/SLA gap, already diagnosed via a stalled-vs-progressed cohort comparison). Chosen over Referral — a similar-strength long-shot — because it fills a funnel stage none of the other two initiatives touch, giving the full three-initiative set top-to-bottom coverage: Lead→PQL, PQL→Trial, Trial→Paid.

Considered, not selected

Mid-Market, meeting vs no meeting

Pool 10/qtr · Gap 40.0pp · p = 0.197 · $5,272/qtr · 17 quarters

The weakest current evidence of everything tested this session — p=0.197 means this could easily just be noise, on a sample of only 5 vs. 5. Once Mid-Market PQL→Trial turned out to be a stronger, better-evidenced way to represent that segment, this became redundant with it. Flagged to monitor, not promoted.

Referral, Trial→Paid

Pool 11/qtr · Gap 23.9pp · p = 0.135 · $2,336/qtr · 19 quarters

A perfectly reasonable hypothesis with a real mechanism (a post-trial onboarding gap), but weaker evidence than Mid-Market PQL→Trial and roughly half the dollar impact. It would have also duplicated the Trial→Paid stage already covered by SMB activation, leaving PQL→Trial as the only major stage without a champion — Mid-Market closes that gap instead.

Roofing Community — Trial→Paid & Lead→PQL

Pool 10 & 31/qtr · Gaps 25.5pp & 13.9pp · p = 0.125 & 0.131 · $34,960 & $30,053/qtr — the largest raw dollar potential of anything found

The biggest opportunity in the entire dataset by dollar impact — and the one thing that disqualifies it anyway: no identifiable mechanism. Checked against every behavioral variable available (activation, sales-assist, engagement, company size), and none of them explain why this channel converts so much better — if anything, it looks slightly less engaged on paper. That's not a problem more data would fix; it's a missing-diagnosis problem. Flagged for a qualitative follow-up (talk to a few customers, review the partner program terms) rather than proposed as a pilot.

Based on this quarter's 260-lead export. "Half the observed gap" and "quarters to prove" use the same conservative method across every row so the comparisons are apples-to-apples.
