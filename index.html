Checking many cuts of the data and reporting the one that looks best tends to make the result look stronger than it really is — check this box if that's how you got here, and the read above will account for it.

Treat the confidence above as more optimistic than it looks. Scanning several candidate patterns and reporting the best one inflates that one systematically — even under pure noise, one of several candidates will look good by chance. Discount it a notch, and prefer a follow-up test on this one pattern alone.
